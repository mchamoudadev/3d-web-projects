# 9 Bar espresso with barista and customer

## How it was made

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, React Three Fiber, Drei, Three.js, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis, Zustand and Tailwind. Geometry, materials and locally packaged fonts form a procedural cafe with complete stylized barista and customer figures.

One global scroll value drives camera movement, prop paths and hand targets: grinding, tamping, locking the portafilter, brewing, steaming, pouring, serving and drinking. Reverse scroll rewinds the same deterministic paths. The cup stays consistent through the handoff, and its liquid counter-rotates during the sip.

This implements the revised visible-people experience. It does not complete the original machine-interior journey, photorealistic models, final commerce ending, cinematic depth of field or full preloader. Original notes record four choreography tests and browser captures; real-device performance and full collision validation remain unestablished.
