# 9 Bar people resources

BRIEF.md preserves the original specification; PROJECT.md and README.md describe the revised implementation. config/timeline.ts and config/choreography.ts define timing, copy, camera and prop paths. components/Set creates the people, cafe, machine, props and effects procedurally. hooks contains arm posing and named-part registration. tests contains choreography verification.

No external GLB or portrait asset is needed. Fraunces and JetBrains Mono fonts come from the npm dependencies and retain their distributed licenses. Earlier browser screenshots mentioned in README.md were local review output and are not bundled. The public directory is intentionally empty; procedural assets are source code.
