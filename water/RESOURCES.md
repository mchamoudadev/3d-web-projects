# Slice resources

- public/frames: full desktop/mobile WebP sequences and manifest; used directly by the browser.
- public/stills: seven optimized poster frames.
- media/clips/clip-01.mp4 through clip-06.mp4: six selected source clips.
- media/slice-story.mp4: joined 48.25-second film.
- media/stills: locally retained originals, crops and candidates.
- docs/storyboard.md: exact supplied storyboard and copy.
- docs/key-still-candidates.json and docs/end-frame-chain.json: generation prompts, decisions and parent/child references.
- content/video-production.json: selected video prompts, seed controls and reviewed hashes.
- docs/media-report.json and docs/production-notes.md: costs, checks and known compromises.

Original provider jobs, discarded video attempts, deployment packages and bulky review captures are excluded. Some historical prompt records reference the original generation directory; only files physically present here are bundled. Recreate contact sheets with scripts/review-video.mjs; creating replacement media requires the original review process. No new media license is asserted by collecting these assets.
